# Citizenship Test Practice

A practice app for the U.S. naturalization test. Open it on a phone and add it to the home screen.

- **Civics:** the official 2008 test (100 questions, 10 asked, 6 to pass), with a 65/20 option. Switch to the 2025 test (128 questions) in Settings. Questions you miss come back more often.
- **Reading and writing:** practice sentences that use only words from the USCIS vocabulary lists.
- **Interview questions:** common questions about your application (Form N-400), with simple explanations of hard words.

Some answers change with elections. Check them at https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before the interview.
